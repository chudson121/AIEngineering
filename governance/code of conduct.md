# AI Code of Conduct

[EU AI Code of Conduct](https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/99641) for businesses:

1. **Risk Evaluation & Mitigation**: Businesses must identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks throughout the AI lifecycle, from development to deployment. This includes diverse testing measures and ensuring traceability of datasets, processes, and decisions.
    
2. **Monitoring Post-Deployment**: Businesses should monitor AI systems after deployment for vulnerabilities, incidents, emerging risks, and misuse, and take appropriate action to address these.
    
3. **Transparency & Accountability**: Businesses should publicly report their AI systems' capabilities, limitations, and appropriate use domains to increase transparency and accountability.
    
4. **Information Sharing**: Businesses should responsibly share information, including evaluation reports and information on security and safety risks, with relevant stakeholders.
    
5. **AI Governance & Risk Management**: Businesses should develop, implement, and disclose AI governance and risk management policies, including privacy policies and mitigation measures.
    
6. **Robust Security Controls**: Businesses should invest in and implement robust security controls, including physical security, cybersecurity, and insider threat safeguards.
    
7. **Content Authentication**: Where technically feasible, businesses should develop and deploy reliable content authentication and provenance mechanisms, such as watermarking, to enable users to identify AI-generated content.
    
8. **Risk Mitigation Research**: Businesses should prioritize research to mitigate societal, safety, and security risks, and prioritize investment in effective mitigation measures.
    
9. **Addressing Global Challenges**: Businesses should prioritize the development of advanced AI systems to address the world’s greatest challenges, in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.
    
10. **International Technical Standards**: Businesses should advance the development of and, where appropriate, adopt international technical standards.
    
11. **Data Management & Protections**: Businesses should implement appropriate data input measures and protections for personal data and intellectual property, and manage data quality to mitigate against harmful biases.
